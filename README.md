# PRV FUND HOUSE

PRV Fund House is a structured research and investment framework built around multiple specialized PRV strategy funds.

## Objective

The purpose of PRV Fund House is to organize stock research into separate strategy funds, with each fund having its own:

- Investment objective
- Research framework
- Stock-selection formula
- Risk and sell discipline
- Valuation framework
- Earnings and growth analysis
- Institutional activity analysis
- Portfolio construction rules

## PRV Research Philosophy

Every stock is evaluated through a combination of:

- Sales growth
- Profit growth
- EBITDA / margin improvement
- ROCE and ROE
- Cash-flow generation
- Balance-sheet strength
- Promoter seriousness
- FII / DII / MF holding and changes
- Capex and CWIP
- Order book and order visibility
- Future capacity expansion
- Earnings estimates and revisions
- Valuation
- Price action and momentum
- Sector rotation
- Risk management and sell discipline

## PRV Fund Structure

Each PRV fund operates independently according to its own objective and formula.

### 1. PRV Compounder Discovery Fund
Focus: 3–5 year structural compounders.

Framework:
Niche / structural market + scalability + earnings growth + ROCE / ROE improvement + capex / CWIP + order visibility + management quality + cash generation + balance-sheet strength + valuation.

### 2. PRV Institutional Conviction Fund
Focus: Companies showing strong institutional conviction combined with fundamental quality and future scale-up potential.

Framework:
MF / FII / DII conviction + fundamentals + earnings growth + future capacity + scalability + valuation.

### 3. PRV Sector Rotation Early Stage Fund
Focus: Identifying sectors and industries at the beginning of institutional rotation.

Sector qualification requires at least 4 of 6 signals to be positive:

1. Relative strength
2. Earnings growth / revisions
3. FII / DII / MF flows
4. Sector breadth
5. Volume / price accumulation
6. Valuation

5/6 = Strong rotation
6/6 = Institutional rotation

Only after sector qualification are industries and stocks selected.

### 4. PRV Swing Core Fund
Focus: High-quality swing opportunities with a 1–6 week horizon.

Framework:
Fundamental quality + price momentum + volume / delivery + breakout proximity + support / resistance + risk-reward + clear invalidation level.

### 5. PRV Value / Correction Accumulation Fund
Focus: Quality companies becoming attractive during market corrections.

Framework:
Valuation reset + earnings thesis intact + ROCE / cash flow + balance-sheet quality + institutional trend + technical support.

A stock being on the watchlist is not an automatic buy signal.

### 6. PRV Growth Fund
Focus: High-growth companies with sustainable earnings and future scalability.

Framework:
Sales growth + profit growth + EBITDA expansion + ROCE / ROE improvement + capacity expansion + order visibility + operating leverage + cash generation + valuation.

### 7. PRV Turnaround / Re-rating Fund
Focus: Businesses showing measurable fundamental improvement and potential re-rating.

Framework:
Earnings recovery + margin improvement + debt reduction / balance-sheet improvement + promoter seriousness + order book + capacity utilization + cash-flow improvement + valuation re-rating potential.

### 8. PRV Special Situations / Opportunity Fund
Focus: Emerging opportunities created by structural changes, new capacity, corporate actions, industry transitions or other identifiable catalysts.

Framework:
Catalyst + business improvement + earnings visibility + balance-sheet strength + valuation + institutional participation + risk/reward.

## Risk Management

Every PRV research call should define:

- Entry / accumulation zone
- Target or expected outcome
- Stop / invalidation level
- Time horizon
- Key thesis
- Key risks
- Sell / exit conditions

## Research Accuracy

PRV research accuracy is tracked separately from portfolio returns.

Each research call is classified as:

- Correct
- Partial
- Wrong

Accuracy is reviewed on a rolling basis using:

- Direction correctness
- Target / zone achievement
- Stop / invalidation outcome
- Earnings thesis correctness
- Time-horizon outcome

## Portfolio Construction

PRV does not treat every qualifying stock as an automatic buy.

Final portfolio decisions consider:

- Conviction
- Risk/reward
- Valuation
- Position sizing
- Sector concentration
- Correlation
- Liquidity
- Market regime
- Portfolio-level risk

## Watchlist Discipline

A PRV watchlist is a research universe, not an automatic buying list.

Stocks are re-evaluated when:

- Earnings change
- Valuation changes
- Institutional holdings change
- Sector rotation changes
- Market corrections occur
- Business thesis changes
- Technical structure changes

## Philosophy

PRV Fund House follows a disciplined approach:

RESEARCH → QUALIFY → RANK → WATCH → RECHECK → ACCUMULATE → MONITOR → EXIT

The objective is not to predict every market move.

The objective is to identify high-quality opportunities, manage risk, and continuously improve research accuracy.
